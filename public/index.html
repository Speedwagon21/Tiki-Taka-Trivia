Tiki-Taka, a live football quiz

3 questions from easy to hard, 10 seconds each, and a faster right answer scores more. Play a room of up to 8 friends or one opponent; the top score takes the pot.

Start a matchSee a match

A match is three questions. Easy, medium, then hard.

The 10-second clock

Four answers and one clock. Tap one, or press 1 to 4 on a keyboard.

The game marks the right answer

The right answer lights up with your points and how many players picked each one.

The pot pays out after 3 questions

The top score wins it. A shared top score splits it, and a dead heat refunds everyone.

The questions

Each match draws one question from each tier of a 48-question bank, skips the ones you saw last time, and keeps every answer on the server until the clock runs out.

Group rooms and head to head

Both play the same three questions, for Gold Coins or Sweeps Coins.

Group rooms for up to 8

Create a room, send the five-letter code or the link, and start when everyone is in.

Head-to-head games for 2

Get matched with a player at the same stake, or send a challenge link to a friend.

Questions

What players ask before their first match.

Gold Coins and Sweeps Coins in the game

Gold Coins are for fun and never cash out. Sweeps Coins are free: 2 on sign-up, 1 a day, and a bonus with each Gold Coin pack.

Wallets for the game

You need none to play. Phantom is only for buying Gold Coin packs.

Points: 500 to 1,000 a question

A right answer scores 500 points plus up to 500 more for speed. A wrong or missed answer scores nothing.

Leaving a game early

Refresh and you keep your seat. Leave and your stake stays in the pot.

Who can play the game

Players 18 or older, where staking is lawful.

New accounts start with 10,000 Gold Coins, on a phone or a laptop.
